Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026 · Last updated: 4 November 2026 · Version 2.0

This Privacy Policy explains how [[COMPANY_NAME]] (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, accesses, uses, stores, shares and deletes information in connection with the Ad Data Insights application and this website (together, the “Service”).

Ad Data Insights is a reporting and analytics tool for advertising accounts. It connects to TikTok Ads through TikTok's official Marketing API (also called the TikTok API for Business) only after an authorized user grants permission, and it uses that access to retrieve, organize and analyze advertising performance data for the connected advertiser account.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to:

It does not apply to TikTok, Google, Meta or any other third-party platform. Those platforms process information under their own privacy policies and terms, and we do not control their practices.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information you give us

  • Contact details — name, work email address, company name and any message content when you contact us, request a demo or ask for support.
  • Account details — the name and email address of the person who creates a workspace and the users they invite, where the Service provides user accounts.
  • Billing details — where a paid plan is purchased, billing contact and tax information. Card payments are processed by our payment provider; we do not store full card numbers.

3.2 TikTok authorization and account data

When you connect an advertiser account, TikTok's authorization flow returns:

  • the advertiser account ID and account name, and the Business Center ID where applicable;
  • the TikTok user ID of the person who authorized the connection;
  • OAuth 2.0 access and refresh tokens, the permission scopes granted, and the authorization and expiry times.

We never receive or store your TikTok password, and we do not ask for it. Authorization takes place on TikTok's own domain.

3.3 Advertising performance data

For each authorized advertiser account, and only within the scopes granted, we retrieve:

  • campaign, ad group and ad identifiers, names, status and settings;
  • creative metadata associated with those campaigns and ads;
  • performance metrics such as impressions, reach, clicks, spend, conversions, video views and the reporting dates and dimensions attached to them;
  • currency, time zone and attribution settings needed to interpret the metrics correctly.

We do not access TikTok user profiles, direct messages, organic content, follower lists or audience identity data. The Service is a reporting tool, not a TikTok account management or content tool.

3.4 Technical and log data

When you use the website or the application, our hosting and infrastructure providers record standard technical information: IP address, browser type and user agent, request timestamps, the pages or endpoints requested, response status and error details. We use this information for security, abuse prevention and troubleshooting.

3.5 Information we do not collect

  • Special category data such as health, biometric or political opinions.
  • TikTok user personal data, direct messages or organic content.
  • Payment card numbers — these are handled by the payment processor.
  • Precise location data from your device.

10. Security

  • TLS 1.2 or higher for all data in transit between your browser, our servers and the TikTok API.
  • Encryption at rest for stored tokens and reporting data; tokens are held in a dedicated secrets store, not in application logs.
  • Access limited to personnel who need it, protected by multi-factor authentication and reviewed periodically.
  • Customer data is logically separated, and API calls are scoped to the advertiser account that granted access.
  • Logging and monitoring of administrative actions and authentication events.

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a personal data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights, we will notify affected customers and, where required, the competent supervisory authority without undue delay.

12. Revoking access and deleting data

You can stop our access to a TikTok advertiser account at any time:

  1. In TikTok Ads Manager or TikTok Business Center, open the settings for the advertiser account and remove Ad Data Insights from the connected applications or authorized apps.
  2. Or disconnect the account inside Ad Data Insights, where that option is available.
  3. Or email [[EMAIL]] with the advertiser ID and the account email used to authorize the connection.

Once access is revoked, we stop making API calls for that account. We delete the associated tokens within 30 days and the stored reporting data within 30 days, except for records we must keep to comply with law. Step-by-step instructions are on our data deletion page.

Deleting data in Ad Data Insights does not delete anything inside TikTok Ads Manager. Data held by TikTok is governed by TikTok's own privacy policy.
