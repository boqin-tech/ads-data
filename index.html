What we do

We support financial institutions that are building or scaling operations outside mainland China. Our work covers the business, operational and technical layers around the licensed entity, not just a single product.

Business support

Market entry assessment, local operating model and the policies and procedures a new overseas entity needs, including coordination with local advisers.

Operations

Back-office processes, vendor coordination, reporting cadence and the handover between local and head-office teams.

Internet technology

Web and backend systems, integrations, cloud infrastructure and access control for teams launching services in a new market.

Data analysis

Data pipelines, warehousing, management reporting and analysis. Advertising reporting is one small workload within this capability.

How we work

Engagements usually start with the operating model and end with a running function, not a slide deck.

1

Scope

We map the market, the entity and the processes the local team will need, and agree what stays with head office.

2

Build

We put the procedures, systems and data flows in place, and test them with the people who will run them.

3

Operate

We run or support day-to-day operations, reporting and analysis, and adjust as the business grows.

Ad Data Insights

Ad Data Insights is a small reporting module within our data practice. It connects to TikTok Ads through TikTok's official Marketing API, retrieves campaign and performance data for accounts the client authorizes, organizes it and exports reports.

The module is read-only: it does not create, edit or publish ads, and it does not access TikTok user accounts or organic content. Data categories, retention and security are documented in the Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time, with deletion steps on the data deletion page.
