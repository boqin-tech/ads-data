Ad Data Insights: what the application does

Ad Data Insights is our reporting module for TikTok Ads. It is used by advertising account owners and the teams they authorize, does not create, edit or publish ads, and does not access TikTok user accounts or organic content.

Retrieve

Pull advertiser account, campaign, ad group and ad structure together with the performance metrics TikTok exposes through the Marketing API, only for accounts that have authorized the application.

Organize

Normalize metrics and dimensions across accounts and date ranges so that naming differences, currencies and reporting windows do not distort the comparison.

Analyze and report

Build performance reports and trend views, then export them for internal reviews, client reporting or further analysis in your own tools.

How it works

Authorization happens entirely inside TikTok. The application never asks for your TikTok password.

1

Authorize in TikTok

You are redirected to TikTok's authorization page, sign in there, and choose the advertiser account you want to connect. TikTok returns an authorization code to our registered redirect URL.

2

Select the account

We exchange the code for an access token, confirm which advertiser account and permissions were granted, and show them to you before any data is retrieved.

3

Review and export

Data is retrieved on demand or on a schedule you control. Reports can be reviewed in the application and exported. You can disconnect the account at any time.

We do not access TikTok user profiles, direct messages, organic content or follower data. We do not sell, rent or resell advertising data, and we do not use it to build audiences or target ads outside the accounts you connected.

Security and access control

Advertising data belongs to the advertiser. Access is limited to the account that granted it and to the people the customer authorizes.

Encrypted in transit and at rest

TLS for all connections, and encryption at rest for tokens and stored reporting data. Tokens are never written to logs or exposed to the browser.

Least privilege

Only the TikTok scopes required for reporting are requested. Each customer's data is isolated, and staff access is limited, logged and reviewed.

Revocation is immediate

Removing the application in TikTok or disconnecting it in our dashboard stops further API calls. We delete the associated token within 30 days.
