Advertising analytics

Understand your advertising performance with clearer data.

Ad Data Insights is a data utility designed to retrieve, organize, and analyze advertising performance information from advertising platforms that an authorized user connects. It helps users review campaign results and make better-informed decisions.

Explore capabilities Read our Privacy Policy

What the application does

The application is intended for advertising account owners and authorized users who need a more convenient way to work with their advertising data.

Data retrieval

Retrieve advertising performance data through platform APIs after an account owner or authorized user grants the required permissions.

Data organization

Organize retrieved information by dimensions such as account, campaign, ad, date, and available performance metrics.

Reporting and analysis

Support performance reporting and analysis to help users understand trends and evaluate advertising results.

Privacy and authorized access

The application is intended to access only the advertising data and permissions needed for its stated functions. Users should connect only accounts they own or are authorized to manage.

The application does not claim affiliation with or endorsement by Google, Meta, or any other advertising platform. Each platform's own terms and permissions continue to apply.
