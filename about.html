Company

We are a technology company.

播琴科技有限公司 is a technology company. We build software that helps advertisers and agencies understand how their paid campaigns perform. Ad Data Insights is our reporting application for TikTok Ads, built on TikTok's official Marketing API.

What we do

Advertising platforms expose a great deal of performance data, but it usually arrives split across accounts, interfaces and export files. Our work is to retrieve that data with the account owner's authorization, keep it organized, and present it in a form that a media buyer, an analyst or a client can actually use.

We focus on read-only reporting. We do not create, edit or publish advertising campaigns on behalf of customers, and we do not act as an advertising agency. Our customers remain in control of their advertising accounts at all times.

Who we serve

Advertisers that run TikTok Ads directly, and agencies that manage advertising for client accounts with the client's permission. Every connection requires the account owner to complete TikTok's authorization flow and grant the required permissions.
