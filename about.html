Company

We help financial institutions go global.

Boqin Technology Co., Ltd. is a technology company. We provide business, operations, internet technology and data analysis capabilities to Chinese financial companies building and scaling operations outside mainland China. Advertising data reporting — the work behind Ad Data Insights — is one of our modules.

What we do

Our clients are financial institutions and fintech teams moving into new markets. The work usually spans four areas, scoped to what the client already has in place.

Business support

Market entry assessment, the local operating model, entity-level policies and procedures, and coordination with local legal, tax and audit advisers.

Operations

Back-office processes, vendor coordination, reporting cadence, controls and the handover between local and head-office teams.

Internet technology

Web and backend systems, integrations, cloud infrastructure and access control — built to launch quickly and stay maintainable.

Data analysis

Data pipelines, warehousing, management reporting and analysis. Advertising reporting is one small workload within this area, delivered through Ad Data Insights.

Who we serve

Chinese financial institutions and their overseas entities, together with fintech teams preparing to operate in a new market. Engagements are scoped to the capabilities a client needs — business, operations, technology or data — rather than a single fixed product.
