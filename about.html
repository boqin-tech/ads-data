Company

We help financial institutions go global.

Boqin Technology Co., Ltd. is a technology company. We provide business, operations, internet technology and data analysis capabilities to Chinese financial companies building and scaling operations outside mainland China. Advertising data reporting — the work behind Ad Data Insights — is one of our modules.

What we do

Our clients are financial institutions and fintech teams moving into new markets. We work on the operating model around the licensed business: market entry, processes, vendor coordination and the reporting that local teams and head office depend on.

We also build and run the technology layer — web systems, integrations and data pipelines — and provide the analysis that turns operational data into decisions.

Advertising data retrieval and analysis is a small part of this work. Ad Data Insights is the module we use for TikTok Ads reporting; it does not define the company.

Who we serve

Chinese financial institutions and their overseas entities, together with fintech teams preparing to operate in a new market. Engagements are scoped to the capabilities a client needs — business, operations, technology or data — rather than a single fixed product.
